  - For a vector, all the invariants under rotation (change of coordinates) can be expressed as function of the vector's magnitude. i.e. magnitude of a vector is the basis for invariants
  - For a second order tensor (i.e. matrix), its eigenvalues are the
    basis of the invariants. This is why I think eignevalues come up
    so often everywhere. Because they are independent of the
    coordinates system we take.
