#+title: Uncertainty
#+date: [2022-05-04 Wed]


- [[id:9B467507-39E7-4F55-B03F-AD53694DBCFE][Epistemic Uncertainty]] is the uncertainty due to lack of knowledge,

  In machine learning connection to Bayesian inference, an approach to
  model epistemic uncertainity is to draw samples from posterior
  predictive distribution which marginalizes over some posterior
  uncertainity about the model. In simple terms, it means that we can
  train/fit multiple models on the dataset and then draw samples from
  those models.

- Aliatoric Uncertainity (Aliator is latin for dice player) is the uncertainity due to inherent randomness
